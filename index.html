Energy Consumption in Australia

Household appliances account for a significant portion of energy use in the Australian market. Refrigerators, washing machines, and televisions combined can represent up to 30% of a typical household's electricity consumption. Choosing energy-efficient appliances is crucial for reducing carbon footprints and lowering utility bills across Australia.
